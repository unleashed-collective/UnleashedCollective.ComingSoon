The idea

Two words, one leash.

Unleashed is the freedom a good walk gives a dog — off the couch, off the schedule, off the leash the second it's safe to be. Collective is everyone that freedom pulls together: the owners who compare notes at the park, the dogs who remember each other's names before their people do.

We design harnesses for that in-between moment — considered enough for the city, sturdy enough for wherever the walk actually goes.
